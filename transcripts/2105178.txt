### 2. Benefits of the Current Image-Based Approach in EDA

| Benefit | Why It Matters for Math & Bangla Scans |
| :--- | :--- |

## 1. Word Count Estimation in Mathematical Documents

### The Technical Challenge
In standard literary texts, word count is simple ($\text{split by whitespace}$). However, in an NCTB mathematics textbook, content falls into three distinct structural categories:

├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Algebraic Expressions     : x^2 + 2xy + y^2 = (x+y)^2               │
│    -> Dense symbols, fractions, and indices. In RAG tokenizers, each   │
│       variable, operator (+, -, =), and exponent is a distinct token. │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Geometric Figures/Labels : Diagrams with embedded text (A, B, C, θ) │
│    -> Off-the-shelf line profilers count drawing strokes as lines.    │
